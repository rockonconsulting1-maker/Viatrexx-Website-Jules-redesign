Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services.

What are the minimum order quantities for manufacturing services?

Our minimum order quantities vary by product type and complexity. For most standard products, our MOQs start at 500 units, while custom formulations may require higher minimums. We're happy to discuss your specific needs and find a solution that works for your business scale.

How long does the manufacturing process typically take?

The timeline varies based on product complexity, order volume, and current production schedule. Generally, standard products require 4-8 weeks from order to delivery. Custom formulations typically take 8-12 weeks, including development and testing phases. We provide detailed timelines during the initial consultation.

What makes the EZ Heartbeat® manufacturing process unique?

The proprietary EZ Heartbeat® process mimics the natural rhythm of the human heartbeat during product preparation, which we've found maximizes cellular response to ingredients. This process includes specialized water structuring, precise timing of ingredient addition, and energetic protection via our Faraday cage manufacturing environment. Independent testing has shown enhanced bioavailability and efficacy compared to conventional methods.

Do you offer product formulation services?

Yes, our product development consulting services include custom formulation. Our scientific team can develop new formulations based on your specific requirements or optimize existing formulations for improved efficacy, stability, or taste. We take a science-based approach, focusing on ingredient synergy and addressing root causes of health issues.

What regulatory support do you provide for private label products?

We provide comprehensive regulatory guidance for all private label products, including assistance with label compliance, claims substantiation, ingredient documentation, and GMP certification. While we advise on regulatory requirements in North America, clients are ultimately responsible for ensuring compliance in their specific markets. We can recommend specialized regulatory consultants for complex international requirements if needed.

How do I qualify for the practitioner education program?

Our practitioner education program is available to licensed healthcare professionals, including naturopathic doctors, chiropractors, medical doctors, osteopaths, acupuncturists, registered nutritionists, and other qualified practitioners. Verification of credentials is required upon registration. We offer different tiers of access based on your practice needs and volume.

Can you help with ingredient sourcing for custom formulations?

Yes, we have an extensive network of trusted ingredient suppliers and can source high-quality raw materials for your custom formulations. All ingredients undergo rigorous quality testing before use. We prioritize sustainable, clean ingredients and can accommodate specific requests for organic, non-GMO, or specialty ingredients subject to availability and minimum order requirements.
