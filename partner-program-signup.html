Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our partnership program.

How long does the application process take?

Typically, the application review process takes 3-5 business days. Once approved, your account can be set up within 24 hours, and you'll receive welcome materials and login credentials to access the partner portal.

What credentials are required for the Practitioner Program?

We require proof of current licensure in your field (MD, ND, DC, LAc, RN, etc.), a professional website or clinic information, and a business email address. Additional documentation may be requested during the verification process.

What is the HPC Community and how do I access it?

The Healthcare Practitioner Community (HPC) is an exclusive online platform for Viatrexx Bio. practitioner partners. It features discussion forums, case studies, webinars, and direct access to our experts. Once your practitioner application is approved, you'll receive login credentials to access the community through our partner portal.

Still have questions?

Contact our partnership team directly for personalized assistance with your specific questions or unique situation.
