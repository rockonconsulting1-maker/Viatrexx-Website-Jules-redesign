# Information Architecture Audit & Rebuild Plan

## 1. Executive Summary
The current site is a static mirror of a LeadConnector funnel, resulting in a deeply nested and redundant directory structure. This plan outlines a flattened, SEO-friendly hierarchy that adheres to the **3-Click Rule** (max depth of 3) and prepares the site for migration to a modern Next.js framework.

## 2. Proposed Hierarchy (3-Click Rule)
The following tree represents the improved, flattened structure. All key conversion pages and products are accessible within 2-3 clicks from the homepage.

```text
/ (Home)
├── about
├── contact
├── science
├── services
├── resources
├── products (List)
│   ├── collections
│   │   ├── cardiovascular-support
│   │   ├── detox-drainage
│   │   ├── general-wellness-neurological
│   │   ├── immune-allergy-support
│   │   ├── metabolic-cellular-health
│   │   ├── microbial-support
│   │   ├── organotherapy-hormonal-support
│   │   ├── pain-inflammation
│   │   └── structural-joint-support
│   └── [product-id] (e.g., aap-im-1, arthrexx, etc.)
└── legal (Footer Group)
    ├── shipping-policy
    ├── refund-policy
    ├── copyright-policy
    └── medical-disclaimer
```
